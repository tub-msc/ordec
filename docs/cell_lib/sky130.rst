:mod:`ordec.lib.sky130` --- SkyWater 130 nm technology
======================================================

This cell library provides part of the `SkyWater Open Source PDK <https://github.com/google/skywater-pdk/>`_.

Substrate handling in LVS
-------------------------

The SKY130 KLayout LVS deck models the substrate as a chip-wide global net
whose name is given by the ``substrate_net`` argument of :func:`run_lvs`
(default ``vss``). Devices with a substrate terminal (e.g. the bulk of
``Nmos``) connect to this global net, and the deck merges it with layout nets
of the same name. The name must therefore match the netlisted name of the net
that ties the substrate taps (``Ptap``), otherwise the bulk terminals remain
on a separate net and the comparison fails.

DRC coverage
------------

The PDK's KLayout DRC deck (``sky130A_mr.drc``) covers widths, spacings and
BEOL via enclosures, but omits several front-end rules (licon, npc, implant
and nwell enclosures, poly/diff gate extensions), and the PDK has no KLayout
antenna deck. :func:`run_drc` runs the PDK's decks only, so it checks none of
these. ``density=True`` runs the PDK's metal density deck, which only checks
the maximum density. :func:`run_magic_drc` runs the full DRC of the PDK's
magic technology (``sky130A.tech``), which has the licon, implant and nwell
rules, the gate extensions and the latch-up rules. It checks neither npc,
whose layer magic ignores when reading GDS, nor antennas, so a tapeout still
needs an independent DRC run. It reports licon.5a on the contacts of the LVT,
NVT and HVT diodes, which magic reads from GDS as plain diode contacts: the
copyup rules of the GDS input of ``sky130A.tech`` ignore the threshold
implants.

.. _sky130_generator_differences:

Differences from the PDK's device generators
--------------------------------------------

The layouts reproduce the device generators of the PDK's magic technology (``sky130A.tcl``) geometrically, with magic's parameter names and defaults; ``tests/test_sky130_pcells.py`` compares them layer by layer. They differ where the generators conflict with the DRC or LVS decks:

* **Gate met1 pads**: widened to the m1.6 minimum area. ``gate_min_area=False`` draws the generator's pads, which only pass once routed.
* **w** and **l**: magic rounds each half distance to the 5 nm grid, so the odd multiples of 5 nm are drawn 5 nm off and fail LVS. Layouts reject them.
* **Cmim** arrays: spaced to the 1.2 µm of capm.2b. ``deck_spacing=False`` draws the generator's spacing, which fails it.
* **Rpoly** with ``nx`` > 1: the generator's 210 nm between the stripes is raised to the 480 nm of poly.9.
* **RisoPw** snakes with odd ``nx``: the guard ring clears the last end's well, which the generator leaves out of its bounding box, so that its ring fails nwell.6.
* **Snakes**: layouts reject ``w`` > ``l``, for which the generator draws ``w`` = ``l``, and a ``RhighPo0p35`` with ``l`` - ``w`` below 2.16 µm, whose first contact the generator overruns.
* Magic computes distances in floating point, so a few options that land exactly between two grid points end 5 nm off. ORDeC rounds them half away from zero, as magic does for exact values.
* **Source and drain**: ``s`` is on the even regions (``sd[0]``, ``sd[2]``, ...), counted from the left, as in ``ihp130``. In magic it is the odd ones, so its ``viasrc`` is ORDeC's ``viadrn``. With ``doverlap=False`` each finger has two regions of its own, mirrored from finger to finger, so the regions are ``s``, ``d``, ``d``, ``s`` and so on.
